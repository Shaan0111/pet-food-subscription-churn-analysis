

# 📊 Insights & Business Findings

## Overview

This analysis focuses on understanding customer churn, behavior, and revenue patterns in a pet food subscription business. The goal is to identify key issues affecting retention and provide actionable business insights.

---

## 1. Churn Analysis

### High Churn Rate

The overall churn rate is approximately **98%**, with only **3 active customers out of 200**.

**Insight:**
The business is facing a severe retention problem. Most customers do not continue after initial engagement.

---

### Active vs Churned Customers

* Active Customers: 3
* Churned Customers: 197

**Insight:**
The customer base is largely inactive, indicating weak long-term engagement.

---

## 2. Revenue Insights

### Total Revenue

* Total Revenue Generated: ₹594,491

**Insight:**
The business generates strong initial revenue, but retention issues limit long-term growth.

---

### Revenue Loss Due to Churn

* Dog Customers: ₹296,601
* Cat Customers: ₹274,064

**Insight:**
Revenue loss is significant across both segments, with slightly higher impact from dog customers.

---

## 3. Customer Behavior

### Average Orders

* Average Orders per Customer: ~3

**Insight:**
Customers engage for a short period and do not continue long-term.

---

### Early vs Late Churn

* Early (1–2 orders): 96 customers
* Later (>2 orders): 101 customers

**Insight:**
Churn occurs across the entire customer lifecycle, not just during the early stage.

---

### Spending Behavior

* Active Customers Avg Spend: ₹7,942
* Churned Customers Avg Spend: ₹2,896

**Insight:**
Higher-spending customers are more likely to stay, indicating a strong relationship between spending and retention.

---

## 4. High-Value Customers

Top customers spend above ₹6,500 each and contribute significantly to overall revenue.

**Insight:**
A small group of high-value customers drives a large portion of the business, making them critical for retention strategies.

---

## 5. Product (Recipe) Analysis

### Churn by Recipe

* Beef: 149 (highest churn)
* Fish: 143
* Chicken: 134
* Lamb: 131

### Revenue by Recipe

* Beef: ₹163,176 (highest revenue)

### Retention

* Beef & Chicken: Lowest retention
* Fish & Lamb: Better retention

**Insight:**
The Beef recipe attracts customers and generates high revenue but fails to retain them, indicating a gap between initial interest and long-term satisfaction.

---

## 6. Quantity & Pricing

### Quantity Impact

Churn rates remain above 96% across all order quantities.

**Insight:**
Order quantity does not significantly influence retention.

---

### Average Order Value

* AOV: ₹1033

**Insight:**
Pricing appears reasonable; churn is likely driven by product or experience issues rather than cost.

---

## 7. Customer Segmentation

### Churn by Segment

* Low Value: 100% churn
* Medium Value: 100% churn
* High Value: ~57% churn

**Insight:**
Retention improves significantly with higher customer value.

---

### Revenue by Segment

* High Value: ₹507,124
* Low Value: ₹85,367
* Medium Value: ₹2,000

**Insight:**
Revenue is heavily concentrated among high-value customers, making them the most important segment for business success.

---

## Final Conclusion

The analysis reveals that the primary issue is not pricing or order quantity, but **product experience and customer engagement**.

Customers are willing to try the product but do not continue using it, leading to extremely high churn rates.

High-value customers show better retention and contribute the majority of revenue, while low and medium-value customers churn completely.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve Product Quality

   * Re-evaluate Beef and Chicken recipes
   * Collect customer feedback
   * Offer more variety

2. Enhance Customer Onboarding

   * Introduce trial packs
   * Improve initial customer experience
   * Provide clear product guidance

3. Retain High-Value Customers

   * Implement loyalty programs
   * Offer personalized incentives
   * Prioritize engagement

4. Convert Low-Value Customers

   * Offer discounts and bundles
   * Increase early engagement
   * Improve product experience

---

## Key Takeaway

Customer retention is the biggest challenge.
Improving product satisfaction and early customer experience is essential for sustainable business growth.
