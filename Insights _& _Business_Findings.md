## Overview

This analysis evaluates customer churn, behavior, and revenue patterns in a pet food subscription business to identify key drivers of retention and business performance.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Extremely High Churn

Churn rate is approximately 98%, with only 3 active customers out of 200.
This indicates a severe retention issue across the business.

---

### 2. Strong Initial Revenue, Weak Retention

Total revenue generated is ₹594,491, but a significant portion is lost due to churn.
The business attracts customers but fails to retain them.

---

### 3. Short Customer Lifecycle

Customers place an average of 3 orders.
Churn occurs both early and after a few purchases, showing that retention issues exist throughout the lifecycle.

---

### 4. Spending Drives Retention

Active customers spend significantly more (₹7,942) than churned customers (₹2,896).
Higher spending is strongly linked to better retention.

---

### 5. Revenue Concentration

High-value customers contribute ₹507,124, while low and medium-value segments contribute significantly less.
The business heavily depends on a small group of high-value users.

---

### 6. Product Performance Issues

The Beef recipe generates the highest revenue but also has the highest churn and lowest retention.
This suggests strong initial demand but poor long-term satisfaction.

---

### 7. Quantity Does Not Impact Retention

Churn remains consistently high across all order quantities.
Purchase volume does not influence customer retention.

---

### 8. Pricing is Not the Problem

Average order value is ₹1033, indicating reasonable pricing.
Churn is likely driven by product experience rather than cost.

---

## Core Insight

Customers are willing to try the product but do not continue using it.
The primary issue lies in product experience and customer satisfaction, not pricing or order quantity.

---

## Business Recommendations

Improve Product Experience
Focus on improving recipe quality, especially Beef and Chicken, and introduce more variety.

Strengthen Onboarding
Enhance the initial customer experience through trial packs and better guidance.

Retain High-Value Customers
Prioritize engagement and loyalty strategies for high-spending users.

Convert Low-Value Customers
Increase early engagement and offer targeted incentives to improve retention.

---

## Conclusion

The business faces a critical retention challenge.
Sustainable growth depends on improving product satisfaction and converting short-term users into long-term customers.
